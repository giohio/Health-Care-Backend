# MCV (Mean Corpuscular Volume) — Reference Ranges and Interpretation

## Definition

MCV is the average volume of a red blood cell, measured in femtolitres (fL). It is the key parameter for classifying anaemia by RBC size.

## Normal Range

| Population | Normal MCV (fL) |
|---|---|
| Adults (men and women) | 80–100 fL |
| Children 6–12 years | 77–95 fL |
| Infants (6–24 months) | 70–86 fL |
| Neonates | 95–121 fL (physiologically elevated) |

## MCV Classification

| MCV | Category | Key Differential Diagnoses |
|---|---|---|
| < 80 fL | Microcytic | Iron deficiency anaemia, thalassaemia, sideroblastic anaemia, lead poisoning, anaemia of chronic disease (occasionally) |
| 80–100 fL | Normocytic | Acute haemorrhage, haemolysis, aplastic anaemia, anaemia of chronic disease, renal failure, bone marrow infiltration, mixed B12 + iron deficiency (cancels out) |
| > 100 fL | Macrocytic | B12 deficiency, folate deficiency, alcohol use disorder, liver disease, hypothyroidism, drugs (methotrexate, hydroxyurea, azathioprine, zidovudine), myelodysplastic syndrome |

## Differentiating Microcytic Anaemias

| Feature | Iron Deficiency | Thalassaemia Minor | Sideroblastic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ferritin | ↓ (< 15 µg/L) | Normal/↑ | Normal/↑ |
| RDW | ↑ (> 14.5%) | Normal | ↑ |
| RBC count | ↓ | Normal or ↑ (paradox) | ↓ |
| Hb electrophoresis | Normal | ↑ HbA2 (β-thal) or Hb H (HbH disease) | Normal |
| Blood film | Hypochromic, pencil cells, target cells | Target cells, basophilic stippling | Ring sideroblasts (bone marrow) |
| Mentzer index (MCV/RBC) | > 13 → iron deficiency | < 13 → thalassaemia | Variable |

## RDW (Red Cell Distribution Width)

- Normal: 11.5–14.5%
- **↑ RDW + ↓ MCV** = Iron deficiency (most likely)
- **Normal RDW + ↓ MCV** = Thalassaemia trait
- **↑ RDW + ↑ MCV** = B12/folate deficiency, liver disease
- **Normal RDW + ↑ MCV** = Alcohol, hypothyroidism, aplastic anaemia

## Reticulocyte Count Interpretation

- Normal reticulocyte %: 0.5–1.5%
- **Low reticulocyte + anaemia** = Hypoproliferative (iron/B12/folate deficiency, aplastic anaemia, bone marrow failure, renal failure)
- **High reticulocyte + anaemia** = Haemolysis or post-haemorrhage (bone marrow responding)

## Priority Level

- Mild microcytic anaemia (Hb > 10 g/dL): **ROUTINE** (outpatient workup)
- Moderate–severe anaemia (Hb < 8 g/dL): **PRIORITY** (identify cause urgently)
- Suspected haemolytic crisis or aplastic anaemia: **URGENT**
