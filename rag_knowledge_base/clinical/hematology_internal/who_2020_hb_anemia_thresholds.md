# WHO 2020 — Haemoglobin Thresholds for Anaemia Diagnosis

## WHO Definition of Anaemia

Anaemia is defined as a haemoglobin (Hb) concentration below established thresholds that vary by age, sex, and physiological status.

## Haemoglobin Thresholds (g/dL)

| Population Group | Anaemia Threshold (Hb < ) |
|---|---|
| Children 6–59 months | 11.0 g/dL |
| Children 5–11 years | 11.5 g/dL |
| Children 12–14 years | 12.0 g/dL |
| Non-pregnant women ≥ 15 years | 12.0 g/dL |
| Pregnant women | 11.0 g/dL (1st & 3rd trimester); 10.5 g/dL (2nd trimester) |
| Men ≥ 15 years | 13.0 g/dL |
| Elderly (≥ 65 years) | Same as adults; Hb < 12.0 (women), < 13.0 (men) |

## Severity Classification

| Severity | Hb (Non-pregnant adult women) | Hb (Men) |
|---|---|---|
| Mild | 11.0–11.9 g/dL | 11.0–12.9 g/dL |
| Moderate | 8.0–10.9 g/dL | 8.0–10.9 g/dL |
| Severe | < 8.0 g/dL | < 8.0 g/dL |
| Life-threatening | < 6.5 g/dL | < 6.5 g/dL |

## Functional Classification by MCV

| MCV (fL) | Classification | Common Causes |
|---|---|---|
| < 80 | **Microcytic** | Iron deficiency anaemia (commonest), thalassaemia, lead poisoning, sideroblastic |
| 80–100 | **Normocytic** | Anaemia of chronic disease, acute blood loss, haemolysis, bone marrow failure, mixed deficiency |
| > 100 | **Macrocytic** | B12/folate deficiency, alcohol, hypothyroidism, liver disease, drugs (methotrexate, hydroxyurea) |

## Iron Deficiency Anaemia — Lab Findings

| Parameter | Iron Deficiency | Thalassaemia | Anaemia of Chronic Disease |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hb | ↓ | ↓ | ↓ |
| MCV | ↓ < 80 | ↓ (often < 75) | Normal or ↓ |
| Serum ferritin | ↓ < 15 µg/L | Normal or ↑ | Normal or ↑ |
| Serum iron | ↓ | Normal | ↓ |
| TIBC | ↑ | Normal | ↓ |
| Transferrin saturation | ↓ < 20% | Normal | ↓ |
| RDW | ↑ | Normal or slightly ↑ | Normal |

## B12 / Folate Deficiency — Key Points

- Serum B12 < 200 pg/mL (or 148 pmol/L) = deficiency
- Serum folate < 3 ng/mL = deficiency
- Megaloblastic anaemia: hypersegmented neutrophils (≥ 5 lobes in > 5% of neutrophils)
- B12 deficiency: also causes subacute combined degeneration of spinal cord (posterior + lateral columns)
- Folate deficiency: neural tube defects if in pregnancy

## Haemolytic Anaemia Signs

- ↑ Bilirubin (unconjugated), ↑ LDH, ↓ haptoglobin
- Reticulocytosis, polychromasia on blood film
- Causes: hereditary spherocytosis, G6PD deficiency, warm/cold AIHA, microangiopathic (TTP, HUS, DIC)

## Priority Level

- Mild–moderate anaemia, stable: **ROUTINE**
- Severe anaemia (Hb < 8.0), symptomatic: **PRIORITY**
- Life-threatening (Hb < 6.5), haemodynamic compromise: **URGENT**

### Overview

Since the approval by WHO of Xpert® MTB/RIF (Cepheid, Sunnyvale, USA) in 2010, significant additional evidence has been generated on its use as initial test for the diagnosis of TB and rifampicin-resistant TB. In recent months new data on the use of Xpert® MTB/RIF Ultra (Cepheid, Sunnyvale, USA) and on the latest version Truenat® MTB and MTB Plus system (Molbio Diagnostics, Goa, India) have also become available.

WHO commissioned a systematic review of all the available data in 2019. The results were assessed during a meeting of an independent WHO-convened Guideline Development Group (GDG) on 3-6 December 2019. Detailed recommendations will be published as part of updated WHO Consolidated Guidelines on TB diagnosis in 2020.

This Rapid Communication aims to inform national TB programmes and other stakeholders about the key implications of the latest evidence on the use of specific molecular assays as initial diagnostic tests of pulmonary and extrapulmonary TB and RR-TB, in adults and children.
