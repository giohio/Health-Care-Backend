**Bronchopneumonia** is a subtype of [pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia "Pneumonia"). It is the acute [inflammation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inflammation "Inflammation") of the [bronchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchus "Bronchus"), accompanied by inflamed patches in the nearby lobules of the lungs.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-1)

It is often contrasted with [lobar pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobar_pneumonia "Lobar pneumonia"); but, in clinical practice, the types are difficult to apply, as the patterns usually overlap.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-Weisenberg2011-2) Bronchopneumonia (lobular) often leads to lobar pneumonia as the infection progresses. The same organism may cause one type of pneumonia in one patient, and another in a different patient.

[![Image 8](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6e/X-ray_of_bronchopneumonia.png/250px-X-ray_of_bronchopneumonia.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:X-ray_of_bronchopneumonia.png)

X-ray of bronchopneumonia: multifocal lung consolidation bilaterally.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-3)

## Causes

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bronchopneumonia&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Causes")]

It is more commonly a [hospital-acquired pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital-acquired_pneumonia "Hospital-acquired pneumonia") than a [community-acquired pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community-acquired_pneumonia "Community-acquired pneumonia"), in contrast to [lobar pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobar_pneumonia "Lobar pneumonia").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-ReynoldsMcdonald2010-4)

Bronchopneumonia is less likely than [lobar pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobar_pneumonia "Lobar pneumonia") to be associated with _[Streptococcus pneumoniae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streptococcus\_pneumoniae "Streptococcus pneumoniae")_.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-urlLobar_Pneumonia-5) Rather, the bronchopneumonia pattern has been associated mainly with the following: _[Staphylococcus aureus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staphylococcus\_aureus "Staphylococcus aureus")_, _[Klebsiella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klebsiella "Klebsiella")_, _[E. coli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escherichia\_coli "Escherichia coli")_ and _[Pseudomonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudomonas "Pseudomonas")_.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-urlPulmonary_Pathology-6)

## Pathology

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bronchopneumonia&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Pathology")]

[![Image 9](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/34/Histopathology_of_bronchopneumonia.jpg/250px-Histopathology_of_bronchopneumonia.jpg)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Histopathology_of_bronchopneumonia.jpg)

Histopathology of bronchopneumonia, showing [neutrophils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrophil "Neutrophil") filling a [bronchiole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchiole "Bronchiole").

Bronchopneumonia may sometimes be diagnosed after death, during [autopsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autopsy "Autopsy").

On [gross pathology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_pathology "Gross pathology") there are typically multiple [foci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_(geometry) "Focus (geometry)") of consolidation present in the basal lobes of the [human lung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_lung "Human lung"), often bilateral. These lesions are 2–4 cm in diameter, grey-yellow, dry, often centered on a [bronchiole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchiole "Bronchiole"), poorly delimited, and with the tendency to confluence, especially in children.

[Light microscopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_microscopy "Light microscopy") typically shows neutrophils in bronchi, bronchioles and adjacent alveolar spaces.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronchopneumonia#cite_note-Weisenberg2011-2)

## Symptoms

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bronchopneumonia&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Symptoms")]

Common symptoms include cough (often with mucus), chest pain, fever, headache (lack of oxygen), wheezing, chills, shortness of breath and body aches.

## Treatment

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Bronchopneumonia&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Treatment")]

Further information: [Pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia "Pneumonia")

Compared to [pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pneumonia "Pneumonia") in general, the association between the bronchopneumonia pattern and [hospital-acquired pneumonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hospital-acquired_pneumonia "Hospital-acquired pneumonia") warrants greater consideration of [multiple drug resistance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_drug_resistance "Multiple drug resistance") in the choice of antibiotics.
