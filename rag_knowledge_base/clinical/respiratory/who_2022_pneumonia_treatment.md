# WHO 2022 Guidelines — Pneumonia Treatment

## Pneumonia Classification

### Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP)
Pneumonia acquired outside hospital or within 48h of admission in a patient not previously hospitalised.

### Hospital-Acquired Pneumonia (HAP)
Onset ≥ 48h after hospitalisation in a patient not intubated at time of admission.

### Ventilator-Associated Pneumonia (VAP)
Pneumonia occurring > 48h after endotracheal intubation.

## Diagnostic Criteria

- New or worsening radiological infiltrate PLUS ≥ 2 of:
  - Fever > 38°C or < 36°C
  - WBC > 12,000 or < 4,000 cells/mm³
  - Purulent respiratory secretions
  - New or worsening hypoxaemia (SpO₂ < 92% on room air)

## Common Causative Organisms

| Setting | Common Organisms |
|---|---|
| CAP (outpatient) | *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Mycoplasma pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae* |
| CAP (inpatient) | *S. pneumoniae*, gram-negatives, *Legionella*, *Staphylococcus aureus* |
| HAP/VAP (non-ICU) | Gram-negatives (*Klebsiella*, *E. coli*, *Pseudomonas*) |
| HAP/VAP (ICU/MDR risk) | *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, MRSA, ESBL-producers, *Acinetobacter* |
| Immunocompromised | *Pneumocystis jirovecii* (PCP), *Aspergillus*, CMV |

## WHO Treatment Recommendations by Severity

### Non-Severe CAP (outpatient)
- **First-line:** Amoxicillin 500 mg TID × 5 days
- **If atypical suspected:** Add macrolide (azithromycin 500 mg OD × 3 days)
- **Penicillin allergy:** Doxycycline 100 mg BID × 5–7 days

### Moderate CAP (hospitalised, non-ICU)
- IV/oral amoxicillin-clavulanate + macrolide (azithromycin or clarithromycin)
- Alternative: 3rd-generation cephalosporin (ceftriaxone 1–2 g IV OD) + macrolide
- Respiratory fluoroquinolone (levofloxacin 750 mg OD or moxifloxacin 400 mg OD) if β-lactam allergy

### Severe CAP (ICU)
- Anti-pneumococcal β-lactam (ceftriaxone 2 g IV OD) + macrolide
- If Pseudomonas risk: piperacillin-tazobactam or cefepime + azithromycin + antipseudomonal fluoroquinolone
- If MRSA risk: add vancomycin or linezolid
- Duration: 7–10 days (stable CAP); 15–21 days if Pseudomonas/Legionella

### HAP/VAP
- Tailor to local antibiogram
- Empiric: piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5 g TID IV + vancomycin/linezolid if MRSA risk
- De-escalate after 48–72h based on cultures
- Duration: 7–8 days (VAP); 7–14 days (HAP)

## Supportive Care

- Supplemental O₂: target SpO₂ 92–96% (88–92% in COPD)
- Early mobilisation, oral hydration if tolerated
- Chest physiotherapy in severe/multi-lobar disease
- Corticosteroids: **NOT** routine in CAP; consider in severe CAP with refractory shock

## Prevention

- Pneumococcal vaccination (PCV13 + PPSV23 for adults > 65 or high-risk)
- Annual influenza vaccination
- VAP bundle: head-of-bed elevation 30–45°, oral care with chlorhexidine, daily sedation holds

## Priority Level

- Non-severe CAP (CURB-65 0–1): **ROUTINE**
- Moderate CAP hospitalised: **PRIORITY**
- Severe CAP, sepsis, respiratory failure: **URGENT**
