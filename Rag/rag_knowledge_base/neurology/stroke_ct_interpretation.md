Title: Stroke CT — Interpretation Guide

# Stroke CT: Emergency Interpretation

## Why CT First in Stroke?

Non-contrast CT is the gold standard first imaging in acute stroke because it:
- **Rules out hemorrhage** (critical for thrombolysis eligibility)
- Is **fast** (<5 minutes acquisition)
- Is **widely available** 24/7
- **Excludes hemorrhage mimics** (tumor, abscess)

Time is critical: "Time is brain" — each minute of untreated stroke = 1.9 million neurons lost.

## Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score (ASPECTS)

ASPECTS quantifies early ischemic changes in the MCA territory:

**Divides MCA territory into 10 regions** (score of 10 = normal, 0 = entire MCA territory involved):

### Regions counted on CT (one point subtracted for each):
- **Caudate** (C)
- **Lentiform nucleus** (L) — putamen + globus pallidus
- **Internal capsule** (IC)
- **Insular cortex** (I)
- **M1-M6** (6 cortical regions)

**Interpretation**:
- **ASPECTS ≥ 7**: Good functional outcome likely, eligible for thrombolysis
- **ASPECTS ≤ 3**: Poorer prognosis, higher hemorrhagic risk

## Early Ischemic Changes

### Signs of Early Ischemia (0-6 hours)

1. **Loss of insular ribbon**: Hypodensity obscuring the insular cortex
   - Most sensitive early sign in MCA territory
   - "Holaround the insula" — loss of definition of the extreme/external capsule

2. **Obscuration of lentiform nucleus**: Fade-out of the lateral border of the basal ganglia
   - Loss of distinction between putamen and internal capsule

3. **Dense MCA sign**: Hyperdense (bright) MCA indicating thrombus
   - >8 mm diameter, more dense than contralateral side
   - Highly specific for MCA occlusion

4. **Loss of sulci**: Effaced cerebral sulci in affected territory
   - Mass effect from cytotoxic edema

5. **Focal hypodensity**: Well-defined low-density area in MCA territory
   - If >1/3 MCA territory → higher hemorrhage risk with thrombolysis

### Cortical Signs

- **Cortical swelling**: Gyral swelling and sulcal effacement
- **Focal edema**: Hypodense region respecting vascular territory

## Intracranial Hemorrhage on CT

### Acute Hemorrhage Characteristics
- **Hyperdense** (bright white), well-defined
- **HU values**: 50-70 HU (fresh blood)
- **Halo sign**: Hypodense rim from serum extravasation
- **Mass effect**: Compression of adjacent structures

### Hemorrhage Patterns

| Location | Pattern | Common Etiology |
|---|---|---|
| Basal ganglia | Large, cohesive | Hypertensive hemorrhage |
| Thalamus | Smaller, bilateral | Hypertensive hemorrhage |
| Pons | Central, symmetric | Hypertensive hemorrhage |
| Cerebellum | Variable | Hypertensive, tumor, AVM |
| Lobar | Peripheral, cortical | Amyloid angiopathy, tumor, trauma |
| Subarachnoid | Sulcal hyperdensity | Aneurysm rupture |
| Subdural | Crescent-shaped | Trauma (acute = hyperdense) |
| Epidural | Biconvex, bounded | Arterial (middle meningeal) |

### Hemorrhage Evolution on CT

| Time | Appearance | Density (HU) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 hours | Hyperdense, sharp | 50-70 |
| 3-7 days | Isodense, blurred | 30-40 |
| 1-2 weeks | Hypodense | 20-30 |
| >2 weeks | Hypodense, fading | CSF-like |

## CT Angiography (CTA)

CTA is typically performed immediately after non-contrast CT in stroke protocol:
- **Identifies occluded vessel**: Location and extent of thrombus
- **Collateral assessment**: Circle of Willis patency
- **Dissection**: Carotid/vertebral artery wall flap or hematoma
- **Aneurysm**: Saccular, fusiform, or mycotic
- **Venous sinus thrombosis**: Filling defect in dural venous sinuses

### CTA Source Images Interpretation
- **Hyperdense clot sign**: Visible thrombus in vessel
- **Clot length**: Longer clots associated with poor collateral flow
- **Collateral score**: Better collaterals = better outcomes despite longer occlusion

## Key Decision Points

### Thrombolysis Eligibility (IV tPA)
- **Time window**: Within 4.5 hours of symptom onset
- **CT must exclude hemorrhage** before treatment
- **Early ischemic changes acceptable** if <1/3 MCA territory
- **No absolute contraindication for subtle early changes**

### Thrombectomy Eligibility
- **Large vessel occlusion**: ICA, M1, M2, basilar, vertebral
- **Time window**: Up to 24 hours in selected patients (DAWN/DEFUSE-3 criteria)
- **ASPECTS ≥ 6** typically required
- **CTA confirms occlusion** before intervention
