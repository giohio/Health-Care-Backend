## Full Blood Count (FBC/CBC): Adult Reference Ranges and Interpretation

A full blood count (FBC), also known as a complete blood count (CBC), measures the cellular components of blood. Interpretation requires comparison with age- and sex-adjusted reference ranges.

### Adult Reference Ranges

| Component | Male Reference Range | Female Reference Range | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haemoglobin (Hb) | 130–175 | 115–160 | g/L |
| Red Blood Cells (RBC) | 4.5–5.9 | 3.8–5.2 | ×10¹²/L |
| Haematocrit (HCT) | 0.40–0.52 | 0.36–0.46 | L/L |
| MCV | 80–100 | 80–100 | fL |
| MCH | 27–33 | 27–33 | pg |
| MCHC | 315–360 | 315–360 | g/L |
| White Blood Cells (WBC) | 4.0–11.0 | 4.0–11.0 | ×10⁹/L |
| Platelets | 150–400 | 150–400 | ×10⁹/L |
| Neutrophils | 1.8–7.5 | 1.8–7.5 | ×10⁹/L |
| Lymphocytes | 1.0–4.5 | 1.0–4.5 | ×10⁹/L |

### Anaemia Classification by MCV

| MCV | Classification | Common Causes |
|---|---|---|
| < 80 fL | Microcytic anaemia | Iron deficiency, thalassaemia, sideroblastic anaemia |
| 80–100 fL | Normocytic anaemia | Anaemia of chronic disease, haemolysis, acute blood loss, renal failure |
| > 100 fL | Macrocytic anaemia | B12/folate deficiency, hypothyroidism, liver disease, alcohol, drugs (methotrexate, hydroxyurea) |

### WBC Differential: Abnormalities

| Cell Type | Low (cause) | High (cause) |
|---|---|---|
| Neutrophils | Neutropenia: viral infection, drug toxicity, bone marrow failure | Neutrophilia: bacterial infection, inflammation, steroids, CML |
| Lymphocytes | Lymphopenia: HIV, steroid therapy, chemotherapy | Lymphocytosis: viral infection, CLL, pertussis |
| Eosinophils | Rare | Eosinophilia: allergy, parasites, drug reaction, Addison's |
| Monocytes | Rare | Monocytosis: chronic infection, inflammatory bowel disease, CMML |
| Basophils | Rare | Basophilia: CML, myeloproliferative disorders |

### Platelet Abnormalities

| Finding | Threshold | Common Causes |
|---|---|---|
| Thrombocytopenia | < 150 ×10⁹/L | ITP, heparin-induced, DIC, liver disease, chemotherapy, viral infections |
| Severe thrombocytopenia | < 50 ×10⁹/L | Bleeding risk — consider platelet transfusion threshold |
| Critical thrombocytopenia | < 20 ×10⁹/L | High spontaneous bleeding risk |
| Thrombocytosis | > 400 ×10⁹/L | Reactive (infection, iron deficiency) or primary (essential thrombocythaemia) |
