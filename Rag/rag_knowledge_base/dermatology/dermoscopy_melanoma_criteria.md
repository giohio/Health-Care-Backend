# Dermoscopy of Melanoma — Atypical Pigment Network and Diagnostic Criteria

## Overview

Dermoscopy (dermatoscopy) significantly improves the clinical diagnosis of melanoma compared to naked-eye examination alone. The technique increases diagnostic sensitivity from ~60% to >90% when specific criteria for melanoma are applied. Recognising an **atypical pigment network** is central to dermoscopic diagnosis of melanoma.

## The Pigment Network

The **pigment network** (also called reticular pattern) consists of a grid-like meshwork of pigmented lines over lighter holes, corresponding histologically to melanin in the rete ridges.

| Type | Description | Association |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Typical (regular)** | Uniform grid, evenly distributed, fades at periphery | Benign melanocytic naevus |
| **Atypical (irregular)** | Variable line thickness, irregular mesh size, abrupt peripheral termination | **Melanoma** |

Atypical pigment network in melanoma features:
- Lines of varying width and colour
- Irregular mesh openings
- Abrupt cutoff at lesion edge without gradual fading
- Focally thickened or broken network segments

## Major Dermoscopic Criteria for Melanoma

### 1. Atypical Pigment Network
Irregular, thickened, and asymmetrically distributed reticular pattern. Key melanoma indicator when present in 25–50% of lesion area.

### 2. Regression Structures
- **Peppering (granularity)**: multiple small grey dots/granules — represent melanophages in regressed areas
- **White scar-like areas**: white structureless zones indicating previous regression
- Regression structures indicate prior melanoma activity even when current lesion appears flat

### 3. Irregular Streaks (Pseudopods / Radial Streaming)
- Projections at the periphery of the lesion radiating outward
- Pseudopods: bulbous finger-like extensions
- Radial streaming: parallel lines radiating from lesion edge
- Indicate aggressive radial growth phase of melanoma

### 4. Blue-White Veil
- Irregular, confluent, structureless area of white-blue colour
- Histology: melanin-filled cells in deep dermis covered by compact acanthosis
- Present in 50–70% of invasive melanomas
- Must be distinguished from milia-like cysts (white, round — seborrhoeic keratosis)

### 5. Irregular Dots and Globules
- Asymmetrically distributed round/oval structures
- Multiple colours (black, brown, red)
- Irregular distribution or clustering at periphery → melanoma

### 6. Irregular Blotches
- Dark structureless areas (black or dark brown) with irregular borders
- Located eccentrically within lesion = melanoma indicator

### 7. Atypical Vascular Pattern
- Irregular dotted, linear, or hairpin vessels
- Milky-red background areas
- Indicates invasive growth and neovascularisation

## Dermoscopic Algorithms for Melanoma Detection

### ABCD Rule (Stolz)
- **A**symmetry: 0–2 points
- **B**order: sharp, abrupt at periphery 0–8 points
- **C**olour: number of distinct colours (white, red, light/dark brown, blue-grey, black) 0–6 points
- **D**ifferential structures: network, dots, globules, blotches, streaks 0–8 points
- TDS (total dermoscopy score) ≥5.45 = **melanoma suspect**

### 7-Point Checklist
Major criteria (2 points each):
1. Atypical pigment network
2. Blue-white veil
3. Atypical vascular pattern

Minor criteria (1 point each):
4. Irregular streaks
5. Irregular pigmentation (dots/globules)
6. Irregular blotches
7. Regression structures

Score ≥3 → refer/excise for melanoma

### 3-Point Checklist (simplified)
1. Asymmetry of colour and structure
2. Atypical pigment network
3. Blue-white structures (veil or regression)
Score ≥2 → melanoma

## Dermoscopic Subtypes of Melanoma

| Subtype | Key Dermoscopic Finding |
|---------|------------------------|
| Superficial spreading melanoma | Atypical pigment network, multi-colour (≥5), regression |
| Nodular melanoma | Blue-black nodule, frequent blue-white veil, polymorphous vessels |
| Lentigo maligna | Annular-granular pattern, asymmetric follicular openings, dark rhomboidal structures |
| Acral lentiginous melanoma | Irregular parallel ridge pattern (vs parallel furrow in benign acral naevi) |
| Amelanotic melanoma | Polymorphous vessels, milky-red background, minimal pigment |

## Dermoscopic Mimics and Differential Diagnosis

| Finding | Melanoma | Benign Differential |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| Atypical pigment network | Yes | Dysplastic naevus (mild irregularity) |
| Blue-white veil | Yes | Blue naevus (diffuse, no other criteria) |
| Pigment network only | Atypical (melanoma) | Regular (common naevus) |
| Central white area + faint pigment network | No | Dermatofibroma (classic) |
| Milia-like cysts + comedo openings | No | Seborrhoeic keratosis |

## Clinical Practice — When to Excise

Excision indicated if dermoscopy shows:
- Atypical pigment network + ≥1 other major criterion
- Blue-white veil in any location
- Regression structures in lesion >6 mm
- 7-point checklist score ≥3
- Any equivocal finding in patient with history of melanoma
