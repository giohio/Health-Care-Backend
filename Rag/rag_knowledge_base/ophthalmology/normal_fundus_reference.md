# Normal Fundus: Anatomical Reference

## Introduction

Fundus examination (fundoscopy/fundus photography) provides a view of the posterior pole of the eye:
- **Optic disc**: Entry point of retinal vessels
- **Macula**: Central vision, most photoreceptor-dense area
- **Fovea**: Central macula, avascular zone
- **Retinal vasculature**: Arterioles and venules radiating from optic disc

## Anatomical Landmarks

### Optic Disc (Optic Nerve Head)

**Location**: Nasal to center of macula
**Appearance**: 
- **Pink-orange** color (due to choroidal background)
- **Sharp, distinct margins** (temporal margin may be slightly blurred in myopic eyes)
- **Circular or slightly oval** (1.5 x 1.8 mm diameter)
- **Cup-to-disc ratio**: Normal is <0.3 (vertical); >0.6 suggests glaucoma

**Components**:
- **Neuro-retinal rim**: Pink neural tissue surrounding the cup
  - ISNT rule: Normally thickest at inferior, then superior, nasal, temporal
  - Focal thinning = glaucoma sign
- **Optic cup**: Central pale area (vessel-free zone)
- **Cup-to-disc ratio: <0.3 normal, >0.6 suspicious for glaucoma

### Retinal Vessels

**Arteries**: 
- **Color**: Light red to orange-red (due to oxygenated hemoglobin)
- **Appearance**: Narrower, straighter, less reflective
- **Wall**: Visible as light reflex (central stripe)

**Veins**:
- **Color**: Dark red to purple (deoxygenated hemoglobin)
- **Appearance**: Wider, more tortuous than arteries
- **Artery:Vein ratio**: Normal = 2:3 or 3:4

**Branch pattern**: All vessels branch at锐 angles from optic disc.

### Macula

**Location**: Temporal to optic disc
**Appearance**:
- **Darker red** than surrounding retina (due to luteal pigment)
- **Avascular zone**: Central fovea lacks retinal vessels (visible as dark spot on fluorescein angiography)
- **Foveal reflex**: Light reflex from central fovea (may be absent in elderly)

**Size**: Approximately 1 disc diameter (5.5 mm)

### Retinal Background

- **Uniform orange-red** color (RPE and choroidal vasculature)
- **No lesions, hemorrhages, or exudates**
- **No retinal elevation or detachment**

## Systematic Assessment

### Systematically Evaluate:

1. **Vitreous**: Clarity, hemorrhage, cells, opacities
2. **Optic disc**: Color, margins, cup, vessels entering/exiting
3. **Vessels**: Caliber changes, AV nicking, hemorrhages
4. **Macula**: Foveal reflex, edema, hemorrhages, exudates
5. **Periphery**: Lattice degeneration, tears, holes, pigmentation

## Age-Related Normal Variations

| Age Group | Normal Findings |
|---|---|
| Young (0-30) | Pink disc, sharp margins, no drusen |
| Middle (30-60) | Mild vessel tortuosity variation |
| Elderly (60+) | Drusen common (age-related), vessel narrowing, disc pallor |

## Diabetic Retinopathy Screening Landmarks

When assessing fundus for DR, compare against normal reference:

### Compare These Structures:

| Finding | Normal | Abnormal (DR) |
|---|---|---|
| Microaneurysms | None | Present (dot hemorrhages) |
| Hemorrhages | None | Dot/blot/flame-shaped |
| Hard exudates | None | Yellow waxy deposits |
| Cotton wool spots | None | White fluffy patches |
| IRMA | None | Abnormal vessel loops |
| Neovascularization | None | New vessel growth |
| Macular edema | None | Hard exudates near fovea |
| Vessel beading | None | Venous caliber irregularities |
| Venous loop | None | Doubling back of vein |

### Grading Reference

**No DR** = Normal appearance, no microaneurysms, hemorrhages, or exudates

**Mild NPDR** = Microaneurysms only

**Moderate NPDR** = More than just microaneurysms but less than severe NPDR

**Severe NPDR** (4-2-1 rule):
- Hemorrhages in all 4 quadrants
- Venous beading in 2+ quadrants
- IRMA in 1+ quadrant

**PDR** = Neovascularization (new vessels on disc or elsewhere)

### Key Structures in Fundus Photography

- **Zone 1**: Posterior pole (optic disc, macula, arcades)
- **Zone 2**: Intermediate periphery (major vessels radiate to)
- **Zone 3**: Far periphery (temporal to arcades)
